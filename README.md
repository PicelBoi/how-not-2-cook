# How Not 2 Cook
A game made for Campfire Flagship, a game jam by Hack Club. Sorta like if Cooking Mama met Pizza Tower and that one minigame where you serve Waddle Dees in Kirby and the Forgotten Land.

## Help Wanted
You're an aspiring chef with no sense of direction or skill, and the biggest restaurant in town is hiring! The problem - right now every single food critic in town is coming in with high expectations, and you're caught in the crossfire, trying to serve dishes as good as possible, and as quick as possible. If you don't... you get fired. And the boss dumps you into the garbage dump. Which hurts even more.
